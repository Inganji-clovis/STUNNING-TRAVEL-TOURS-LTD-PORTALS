# User Guide: Stunning Travel & Tours Portal

Welcome to your new standalone portal! This guide will help you get started and manage your daily activities.

---

## 🚀 How to Start the Portal
You don't need to open any complex tools. Simply find the **Globe Icon** on your desktop named **"Stunning Portal"** and double-click it.

- **Wait a moment**: The first time you open it, Docker might take a few seconds to "wake up" the database.
- **If it shows an error**: Just wait 30 seconds and click the **Refresh** button in the window.

## 🔑 Login Credentials
Use the credentials provided by your Administrator. 
- **Username**: `stunningAdmin`
- **Password**: `stunning@Admin2026`

## 👥 Using it on Multiple Computers
If you are the "Master" computer:
1. Ensure your computer is ON and Docker is running.
2. Give your colleagues the IP address of your computer (e.g., `http://192.168.1.10:8080`).

If you are a Staff Member:
1. Open your browser and type the IP address given to you.
2. You can save this as a "Bookmark" for quick access.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting
- **Portal is blank**: Right-click the desktop icon and select "Run with PowerShell" to ensure the services are started.
- **"Docker not found"**: Ensure Docker Desktop is running in your system tray (near the clock).
- **Slow performance**: Ensure you don't have too many other heavy apps (like many Chrome tabs) open at the same time.
